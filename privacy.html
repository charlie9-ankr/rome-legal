Summary

ROME, 161 Brayton, Englewood, New Jersey, USA ("ROME") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Effective October 5, 2026. We do not sell your personal information.

What we collect

Account: name, email, handle, password (stored hashed), profile details you add (bio, city, photo).

Activity and location: your runs, including GPS routes, distance, pace, time, elevation, splits, calories, notes, shoes, feedback, and when and where you ran. Location is collected while you track a run and when you build a route near you. Precise location can reveal where you live, work, and travel.

Health and fitness: data you enter, and, if you choose to connect it, data from Apple Health. We only access Health data you permit.

Social: your friends, crews, messages, goals, achievements, posts, likes, and comments.

Purchases: subscription status and promo or creator code usage. Apple processes payments; we do not receive your card details.

Photos you choose: only photos you pick or take for a run card or post; we never read your photo library otherwise.

Technical: device type, app version, crash and diagnostic data, and IP address.

How we use it

To run ROME: record and show your runs, build routes, power the Trainer and goals, show your social features, process subscriptions and creator rewards, and keep the service secure.

To improve ROME: understand which features work and learn which routes runners like so recommendations get better. We may use de-identified or aggregated data for this.

To communicate with you about your account, safety, and service updates.

AI and service providers

To provide AI coaching and route building we send relevant information (for example your training history, your request, and your approximate location) to AI and mapping providers that process it for us, including Anthropic (Claude, which processes your chat and route requests), Apple (maps and purchases), OpenRouteService / HeiGIT, Valhalla and Overpass (OpenStreetMap-based routing and map data, which receive coordinates), AWS terrain tiles (elevation), our email delivery provider (Resend), RevenueCat (subscription status, if enabled), and Railway (hosting and database). They may only use it to provide their service to us, under their terms.

We do not use your private data to train third-party AI models.

What others can see

Your profile basics (name, handle, city, photo, achievements) may be visible to other users. Runs, routes, goals, and messages are visible only to the people you choose to share them with, such as your crew or followers. Crew goal progress shows totals, not your routes. We recommend hiding the start and end of runs near your home.

Reports, blocks and safety

If you report content or block someone, we store the report (its reason, your note, and a copy of the reported content so it can be reviewed even if it is later deleted) and who you blocked. Your identity is never shown to the person you report. We keep reports for as long as needed to protect the community and meet legal obligations. If a banned person deletes their account we keep a one-way hash of their email address so they cannot re-register.

Sharing and legal

We share data with service providers described above, when you tell us to, to comply with law or protect rights and safety, or in a business transfer. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context advertising.

Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, control what you share, turn off location permission in iOS Settings, disconnect Apple Health, and delete your account and data in the app. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California) you may also have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

Retention and security

You can download a copy of your data from the app (data export). We keep your data while your account is active and delete it when you delete your account (More > Delete account), except where we must keep limited records for legal, security, or accounting reasons. We use industry-standard safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children

ROME is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers, changes, contact

Your data may be processed in countries other than yours, with appropriate safeguards where required. We may update this policy; if changes are material we will notify you in the app. Questions or requests: charlie.hilbert06@gmail.com.
