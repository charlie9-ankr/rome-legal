Be a good teammate

ROME is for runners to encourage each other. Treat people the way you would on a group run. These guidelines apply to everything you post or send: Pulse posts and comments, crew chat, direct messages, names, handles, bios and photos.

What is not allowed

Hate: slurs or attacks on people for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or similar traits.

Harassment and threats: bullying, targeting someone, unwanted contact after they asked you to stop, threats of violence, or encouraging someone to hurt themselves.

Sexual content: nudity, sexual messages, or sexual content involving anyone under 18 (reported to the authorities).

Violence and danger: graphic violence, or content that encourages dangerous or illegal acts.

Spam and scams: junk, repeated messages, fake offers, or links meant to mislead.

Impersonation and privacy: pretending to be someone else, or sharing other people's private information or exact locations without permission.

Filters

ROME automatically blocks some language before it is posted. If something you write is rejected, reword it. If you think it was a mistake, contact us.

Report and block

Tap the ••• on any post, comment, message, profile or crew to Report it or Block the person. Reports are private. The person is never told who reported them.

Blocking hides you and them from each other everywhere: posts, comments, crew chat, messages, search and profiles. They are not notified. Manage blocked accounts in Privacy.

What we do about reports

We review every report within 24 hours. Depending on what we find we may remove the content, send a warning, suspend the account for a period, or remove it permanently. Content that has been reported by several people is hidden automatically until we have reviewed it.

People who break these rules repeatedly, or who seriously harm others, are removed and may not return.
