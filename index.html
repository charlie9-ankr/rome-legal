AI running app

Run ROME.

Big numbers, few buttons. Track runs with GPS, build a route that fits your goal with Sam, your AI coach, and run with your crew.

Just run

Start a run in one tap. Distance, pace and route are recorded, even with the screen locked.

Build my run

Tell Sam what you want. ROME builds a route from real map data. Sam is an AI and can be wrong, so use your own judgment.

Crews

Share runs, set goals and chat with your running crew. Your runs are private by default, with privacy zones around home and work.
