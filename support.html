Support

We read every message. Typical reply within 2 business days; abuse and safety reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Contact

Email charlie.hilbert06@gmail.com. Include the email on your ROME account and, if useful, your app version (More > bottom of the menu) and iPhone model. Never send your password.

Delete your account

In the app: Profile tab > menu (More) > Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account, runs, routes, messages and posts. Deleting the app alone does not delete your account. If you cannot open the app, email us from your account address and we will delete it.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it first in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Export your data

You can download a copy of your data from the app. Or email us and we will send it.

Report abuse or a safety problem

In the app, tap the ••• menu on any post, comment, message, profile or crew and choose Report or Block. Reports are private and are reviewed within 24 hours. You can also email us. Read the Community Guidelines.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

FAQ

How do I cancel ROME Plus?

On your iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > ROME. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open ROME Plus in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Why does ROME want Always location?

So your run keeps recording with the screen locked or ROME in the background. Tracking stops when you end the run. You can use While Using, but you will need to keep the screen on.

Who can see my runs?

Only you, unless you share. Set privacy zones in More > Privacy to hide the start and end of runs near home or work on anything shared.

Is Sam a real person?

No. Sam is an AI coach. It can make mistakes and is not medical advice.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. A code is emailed to you.

My route looked unsafe.

Routes come from map data that can be out of date. Use your own judgment, stop if unsure, and tell us so we can improve.
